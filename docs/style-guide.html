Motion

Calm and slow. Things fade up or slide in once, with a soft landing. Add a class, nothing else:

class="reveal"                 fades up when it scrolls into view
class="reveal reveal--up"      slides up from further down (cards)
style="--i:2"                  stagger: 0, 1, 2, 3 ... one after another
class="split"  + <span>       heading text rises out of a mask
class="reveal-img"             picture wipes open (put the <img> inside)

Do not write your own animations or JavaScript. Hover and active colours are gold. If you want a new effect, ask Obash.

Never do this

No badges or chips, stat rows, testimonials, certifications or invented history. No Swiss or Geneva wording, no CHF, no real company names. Prices in USD. Cities: Abuja, Lagos, Port Harcourt. No "Book appointment" buttons, no currency switcher, no PDF downloads. Corners 6px. One short line of text per card.
