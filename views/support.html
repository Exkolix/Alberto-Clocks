A manual-wind watch needs a gentle turn of the crown each day, stopping at the first resistance. An automatic winds itself as you wear it, and a few turns of the crown gets it going after a rest. Always set the time with the crown pulled out, never while the date is changing late in the evening.

Check the water resistance marked on the dial or case back first. Keep the crown pushed in, avoid hot water and steam, and rinse with fresh water after the sea. Seals age, so have them tested when the watch is serviced.

Mechanical watches are usually serviced every few years so the oils stay fresh. Quartz watches need a new battery when they stop, and a seal check at the same time.

Wipe the charging contacts and use the charger that came with it. Restart the watch and your phone, then pair again from the app. If it still does not respond, bring it to any store.
